    ## United Chinese Professional Associations in Europe (UCPAE)


    The United Chinese Professional Associations in Europe (UCPAE, also known as the European Alliance) is officially registered at the China-Belgium Technology Center in Belgium under registration number 1024.909.225.


    The alliance previously used the name "Federation of Chinese Professional Associations in Europe" and the abbreviation FCPAE. It now uses the name "United Chinese Professional Associations in Europe" and the abbreviation UCPAE. This name update reflects continuity in the alliance's organization and activities; historical materials retain the names used when they were published.


    UCPAE promotes communication and cooperation among its member associations, strengthens connections among professionals, and supports collaboration with universities, research institutions, and industry associations in Europe and China. Its mission is to advance China-Europe cooperation and development in science and technology, education, culture, and industry.

    ## 欧洲华人专业协会联盟（UCPAE）


    欧洲华人专业协会联盟（United Chinese Professional Associations in Europe，简称 UCPAE 或欧联盟）正式注册于比利时中比科技园，注册代码为 1024.909.225。


    本联盟过去使用“全欧华人专业协会联合会”名称及英文简称FCPAE，现统一使用“欧洲华人专业协会联盟”名称及英文简称UCPAE。此次名称更新体现联盟组织和活动的延续；历史资料仍保留发布时使用的名称。


    欧联盟致力于促进会员协会之间的交流与合作，增进专业人士之间的联系与互动，并推动与欧洲及中国高校、科研机构和行业协会的合作，促进中欧在科技、教育、文化及产业等领域的共同发展。
